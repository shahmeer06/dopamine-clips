Privacy Policy

Clipper, the app behind Dopamine Clips · Last updated November 15, 2026

Clipper is a private tool that runs on the Dopamine Clips owner's own computer. It signs in to one YouTube account, the Dopamine Clips channel, which belongs to the person running it. It isn't offered to anyone else and has no other users.

What Clipper accesses through Google

  • Upload videos to YouTube (youtube.upload): to post Shorts to the Dopamine Clips channel.
  • View YouTube Analytics reports (yt-analytics.readonly): to read view counts and watch time for the channel's own Shorts, so it can favour the kinds of clips that perform well.
  • View the YouTube account (youtube.readonly): only to show which channel it's signed in to.

How that information is used and stored

The sign-in token and the channel's view statistics are stored only on the owner's computer. They are used only to upload the channel's own videos and to decide what to clip next. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

To choose the cut and write titles, Clipper sends the transcript of the public source video around the chosen moment to Anthropic's Claude. It doesn't send any data received from Google APIs.

Clipper's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections. Deleting Clipper's folder from the owner's computer deletes everything it stored.

Viewers

Clipper doesn't collect anything about people who watch Dopamine Clips. YouTube's and TikTok's own privacy policies cover viewers on those platforms. This website uses no cookies or tracking.
