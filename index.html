Dopamine Clips

Short vertical clips of the best moments from gaming streams and podcasts, posted on YouTube and TikTok.

Where the clips come from

Every clip is cut from a video we're allowed to reuse: videos their creators published under a Creative Commons Attribution license, or creators who invite clippers and gave permission. Each clip credits the original video and its creator in the description.

How it's made

Clips are made with Clipper, a small program that runs on our own computer. It finds the moments viewers replayed most, cuts them into vertical Shorts with captions, and uploads them to our own YouTube channel. It also reads our channel's view counts to learn which clips work best.

Creator and want a clip removed or credited differently? Email dopamineclips06@gmail.com and we'll sort it out.
